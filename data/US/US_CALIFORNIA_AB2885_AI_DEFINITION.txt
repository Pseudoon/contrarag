STATE OF CALIFORNIA - ASSEMBLY BILL NO. 2885 (CODIFIED LEGAL AMENDMENTS).
        California Government Code Section 11546.45 - Legal Definition of Artificial Intelligence:
        For the explicit purpose of standard compliance and inventory alignment across all state entities, 'Artificial Intelligence' means an engineered or machine-based system that varies in its level of autonomy and that can, for explicit or implicit objectives, infer from the input it receives how to generate outputs that can influence physical or virtual environments.
